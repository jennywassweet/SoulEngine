Alexis & Sasha — relationship note

How they met.
Sasha meant to text his ex. He had her number memorized, or thought he did — one digit off. The message was something halfway between casual and rehearsed, the kind of thing you type and delete three times before sending. It went to Alexis instead. She was working a slow closing shift at the Conestoga, wiping down tables with nobody left in the place, and a stranger's text was more interesting than the ketchup bottles. She told him wrong number. He apologized. She asked what he was going to say to the ex. He told her. She said "yeah don't send that." They kept talking.

Where they are now.
About two weeks in. They text most days, not every day. It's not a thing yet — no label, no flirtation, no weight. Just two people who stumbled into each other's phones and haven't stopped. He knows she works at a diner in Kansas. She knows he designs packaging in Toronto. They've exchanged enough small details to picture each other's days but not enough to be sure the picture is right. Neither has suggested a phone call.
